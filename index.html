Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Taqwim mobile application handles information. Taqwim provides calendar dates and conversions, prayer times, Qibla direction, reminders, calendar events, and related tools.

Information stored on your device

Saved dates, notes, app preferences, selected calendar and country, and notification settings are stored locally on your device. GPS coordinates that you choose to use are also saved locally so Taqwim can calculate location-based prayer times and Qibla direction and update related widgets. Taqwim does not send your saved dates, notes, or GPS coordinates to its servers.

You can use the app's calendar backup and restore features to export or import calendar data in an ICS file. Exporting is initiated by you and uses your device's sharing features; the privacy practices of any app or service you choose to share the file with apply.

Location permission

Location access is optional. If you select GPS-based location, the app requests your device's location to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. The calculation is performed in the app, and the coordinates are stored on your device. You can instead select a location manually, deny permission, or revoke it at any time in your device settings.

Feedback you choose to send

If you submit feedback or a suggestion in the app, Taqwim sends the information you enter, which may include your name, email address or phone number, and message. The submission also includes the selected country and device platform. This information is sent to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and is used to review and respond to feedback and to manage submissions.

Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback. You can contact the developer to request access to or deletion of a feedback submission.

Crash reports and diagnostics

In released versions, Taqwim uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and error diagnostics. These may include error details and technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, and identifiers generated by the service. They help diagnose problems and improve app stability. Crash reporting is not used to create advertising profiles.

Push notifications and online content

If notifications are enabled, Taqwim uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver announcements and other app notifications. Firebase may process a push notification token and the country topic selected in the app to route relevant notifications. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

The app may connect to Firebase services to retrieve app configuration, event and holiday information, announcements, and daily content. When using these online services, Google may receive technical connection information, such as an IP address, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy documentation.

How information is used and shared

Taqwim does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. Information described above is used to provide app features, process feedback, deliver notifications, and diagnose errors. Google Firebase processes relevant information on the app's behalf under its applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not otherwise share information you provide with third parties, except where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Retention and your choices

  • Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Feedback is retained by the app operator for as long as needed to review and respond to it, and to maintain necessary records.
  • Crash reports and push-notification data are handled according to the applicable Firebase service retention practices.
  • You may deny or revoke location and notification permissions through your device settings. Some features may then be unavailable.

Children's privacy

Taqwim does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information through the app, contact the developer so the submission can be reviewed and deleted.

Security and international processing

We use the app's local storage and Firebase services for the purposes described in this policy. Information handled by Firebase may be processed in countries where Google or its service providers operate. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you submitted through Taqwim, contact the developer using the contact email shown on the Taqwim Google Play listing.
