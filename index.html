Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy describes how Taqwim handles information when you use its calendars, prayer times, Qibla, reminders, and related features.

Information on your device

Saved dates, notes, preferences, and GPS coordinates (if you choose GPS location) are stored on your device. GPS is used for prayer-time and Qibla calculations; you can choose a location manually instead. This personal data is not uploaded to Taqwim's servers. You can export or import calendar data using an ICS file; exported files are shared only when you choose to share them.

Information you send

If you submit feedback, the name, contact details, and message you enter, along with your selected country and device platform, are sent to Firebase Cloud Firestore so the developer can review your feedback.

Firebase services and notifications

Taqwim uses Firebase to provide online app content, send announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging, and receive crash diagnostics through Crashlytics. Messaging may process a device token and your selected country to route announcements. Notification permission is controlled through your device's operating system; Taqwim has no in-app setting to turn notifications on or off. Google may process technical connection data when providing these services. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Taqwim does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. Feedback is kept as needed to handle it; local data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. You can manage location and notification permissions in your device settings. You may contact us to request deletion of feedback you submitted.

Children and policy updates

Taqwim does not knowingly request personal information from children. We may update this policy when app practices change; the latest version and effective date will appear on this page.
