Privacy Policy for Taqwim

Last updated: November 2026

Taqwim is designed as a standalone utility offering comprehensive calendar conversions (Hijri Shamsi, Hijri Qamari, and Gregorian), standard event tracking, prayer times, Qibla compass calculations, home screen widgets, and date management tools.

1. Information Handling & On-Device Storage

Your privacy is respected by default. Taqwim does not collect, harvest, track, or share your personal data:

  • Saved Dates & Notes: Any date notes, reminders, or saved events you create are saved strictly inside your phone's local database. They are never transmitted off your device.
  • Date Conversions & Calculations: Tools including date conversion, date differences, and age calculation run entirely on-device using standard mathematical and astronomical calendar formulas.
  • Widgets: Home screen widgets read their display data directly from your device's local storage and settings without external network interaction.

2. Device Permissions & Sensor Use

To enable specific utility features, Taqwim requires certain local permissions:

  • Location: Used strictly in real-time to compute precise prayer times and Qibla direction based on standard astronomical coordinates. Your location is processed on-device and is never logged, stored, or sent to any remote server.
  • Sensors (Compass/Magnetometer): Used solely to orient the Qibla compass pointer toward the Kaaba in real-time.
  • Notifications: Used exclusively to deliver scheduled event reminders and prayer alerts locally on your phone.

3. Cloud Services & App Content

Taqwim uses Google Firebase exclusively as a read-only distribution channel to maintain and update general calendar metadata (such as official UN international observances and local public events in Afghanistan and other supported regions).

No user-generated data, identity tokens, calendar notes, or personal identifiers are uploaded, synchronized, or stored on Firebase servers.

4. Third-Party Access

Taqwim does not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms.

5. Data Deletion

Because all personal records and preferences exist solely within your phone's local storage, you can immediately delete all associated data at any time by clearing the application cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
